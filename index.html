One query, the right entry point

Count commits.
Without losing 11.5% of them.

Filtering raw commits by project name silently drops part of mainline and fabricates a gap in 2022. The curated entry points exist so that the obvious query is also the correct one.

See the six classic traps →
-- non-merge mainline commits per year
SELECT date_trunc('year', author_date) AS year,
       count(*)                        AS commits
FROM   commits.mainline
GROUP  BY 1
ORDER  BY 1;

Open code, protected people

Every collector, cleaning step and matching method is public. The database itself is not distributed, and no person-level data ever leaves it.
